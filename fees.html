Admission Guide

Simple 4-Step Admission Procedure

Enrolling your child at The Ambassadors of Knowledge is straightforward and family-friendly.

1

Step 1: Inquiry

Contact our administration via phone, WhatsApp, or the online form to check seat availability.

2

Step 2: Campus Visit

Visit our school in Dhok Badam Satramile, meet the faculty, and tour our classroom environment.

3

Step 3: Document Check

Submit the student's Form B copy, passport-sized photographs, and parent/guardian CNIC copy.

4

Step 4: Enrollment

Complete the admission form, receive the student booklist/uniform instructions, and start class!

Checklist of Documents Required for Admission:

  • 2 Passport-sized recent photographs of the student
  • Copy of Nadra Form B or Birth Certificate
  • Copy of Father's or Guardian's CNIC
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate / Character Certificate (for Class 1 to 10 transfers)
