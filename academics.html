Academic Excellence

Structured Learning from Playgroup to Matric

We offer a comprehensive academic pathway tailored to the developmental stages of our students, with dedicated support for matriculation Arts subjects in Class 9 and Class 10.

Ages 3 - 5 Years

1. Early Childhood Education (Playgroup, Nursery, Prep)

Monthly Fee: Rs. 1,500

In our Early Childhood section, we introduce little learners to the joy of school through interactive play, tactile crafts, phonetic language exploration, and social bonding.

Language & Phonics

Alphabet recognition in English and Urdu, vocabulary expansion, and cheerful rhymes.

Basic Numeracy

Counting, shapes, patterns, and interactive number puzzles to build mathematical curiosity.

Motor & Social Skills

Pencil grip refinement, colouring, sharing, manners, and cooperative classroom behavior.

Grades 1 to 5

2. Primary School (Classes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

Monthly Fee: Rs. 1,600 - 2,000

Primary grades build essential cognitive competence. Students develop fluent reading, clear writing, numerical problem-solving, and scientific curiosity under watchful mentorship.

Languages (English & Urdu)

Grammar, comprehension, creative essay writing, reading comprehension, and pronunciation.

Mathematics & Science

Arithmetic operations, word problems, geometry, living organisms, nature, and the environment.

Social & Moral Studies

Islamiyat (Nazra Quran, daily duas, ethics), Pakistan geography, history, and civic duties.

Grades 6 to 8

3. Middle School (Classes 6, 7 & 8)

Monthly Fee: Rs. 2,200

Middle school transitions young adolescents into independent, analytical thinkers. Courses expand into rigorous foundational sciences, advanced mathematics, literature, and digital literacy.

Analytical Thinking

Structured problem-solving, science experiments, conceptual explanations, and debating.

Pre-Matric Guidance

Regular chapter-wise assessments to ease transition into secondary board exams.

Moral Leadership

Personal accountability, time management, classroom leadership, and sportsmanship.

Secondary Specialization

4. Matric Wing: Pre-9, Class 9 & Class 10 (Arts Subject Group)

Monthly Fee: Rs. 2,500

At The Ambassadors of Knowledge, 9th and 10th grades are specialized in the Arts Subject Group. We provide an intensive, exam-focused academic regimen tailored to help students secure top division marks in their board examinations.

Core Arts Subjects

  • • English Compulsory
  • • Urdu Compulsory
  • • Islamiyat & Pakistan Studies
  • • General Mathematics
  • • General Science

Humanities Electives

  • • Islamic Studies / History
  • • Civics & Social Sciences
  • • Education / Home Economics
  • • Elective subjects as per Board regulations

Board Prep System

  • • Past 10-year paper solutions
  • • Weekly test sessions & mock exams
  • • Individual feedback on paper presentation
  • • Dedicated pre-board coaching
